📚 Key Vocabulary:

Diploid (2n): Cell has 2 sets of chromosomes (like at the start)
Haploid (n): Cell has 1 set of chromosomes (at the end)
Crossing Over: Chromosomes swap DNA pieces (causes mutations)
Random Segregation: Chromosomes split randomly into cells
Daughter Cells: New cells made after division

Goal: Show how DNA moves from parent cell to offspring cells.
